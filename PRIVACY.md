# Sprout privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

Sprout is an open-source habit tracker for Android, published by Mohd. Nisab ("we"). This policy covers the Sprout app from Google Play, GitHub Releases and F-Droid.

## What Sprout stores

Your habits, check-ins, notes and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We don't run a server and never receive this data.

## What Sprout collects and shares

Nothing. Sprout has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it doesn't share data with anyone.

- The Google Play and F-Droid builds don't connect to the internet.
- The GitHub build connects only to GitHub (`api.github.com` and GitHub's download servers) to check for a new version of Sprout and download it. That request contains no personal data.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: to show the habit reminders you set.
- **Alarms & reminders** (exact alarms): optional, so reminders arrive on time.
- **Run at startup**: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.

## Backups and exports

- **Export** writes a JSON or CSV file wherever you choose, using Android's file picker. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.
- **Android backup**: if your phone's backup is turned on, Android may include Sprout's data in your device backup. Google runs that backup under your Google account and its own privacy policy. Sprout never sees it.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall Sprout, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Sprout → Storage. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children

Sprout isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
